# Camera reference revision R2

[Download the CAD package](Camera_revision_R2.zip?raw=true) · [View the actual CAD geometry](Revised_model_R2_views.png) · [Persian change report](README_R2.md)

R2 rebuilds the marked side panels and replaces the rectangular underside panel with two real circular perforated grilles (61 holes each). The package includes a colored STEP assembly, FreeCAD document, views, construction scripts and verification results.

Independent STEP reimport verified 36 valid positive-volume solids, a 280 × 110 × 110 mm overall envelope and 122 cylindrical grille holes of 1.3 mm diameter. New side panels and trim have no volumetric overlap with the housing core. The profiles, hole locations and unannotated dimensions are visual estimates; native SOLIDWORKS import and manufacturing fit are not verified. R1 remains available in the adjacent folder.
